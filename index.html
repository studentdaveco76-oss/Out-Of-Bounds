Grading guide

5A

Like new

Appears virtually flawless. No scuffs, marks, or discoloration. Full play quality.

4A

Excellent condition

Minimal signs of play. No scuffs, may have faint marks. Excellent play quality.

3A

Good condition

Moderate signs of play. Some scuffs, marks, or discoloration. Good play quality.

2A

Fair condition

Heavy signs of play. Scuffs, marks, and discoloration. May have minor blemishes. Still playable.

1A

Practice / heavily used

Extensive wear and discoloration. Heavy scuffs, cuts, or abrasions. Ideal for practice or range use.
